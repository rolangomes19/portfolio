Overview

Incridea is NMAM Institute of Technology's national-level college fest. As Design Committee Lead, I directed the brand identity and visual themes for the event and owned the full attendee, sponsor, and visitor experience — the fest theme carried across digital, social media, and physical/print media.

I led this alongside freelance graphic design work and my Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering — the same systems-thinking habit that later carried into UX and design systems work started here, in brand identity and event experience design.

Outcome

The fest drove $35k in sponsorship — roughly 10x the historical average — with the brand and creative direction credited as part of what made the pitch land.
