Incridea 2022 - Odyssey of a Lifetime

Incridea is the national level techno-cultural fest of NMAM Institute of Technology. The 2022 edition of Incridea had the theme "Odyssey of a Lifetime", where the idea was an amalgamation of the majesty of the past and the excitement of the future. Visually, we want to express this theme through the concept of portals, linking worlds together. You can see that here through a mystical portal up in the sky, opening up for the great pyramids in it's past.

Fest merch

For participants of the fest, the merch makes them feel belong to the fest and serves as a memorabilia of the event. Our goal with the participant merch was to make something that the person would be comfortable wearing it outside and after the fest as a normal piece, rather than have it become a fest-specific outfit.

Fest mascot and the comic strip

You’ve seen the tease, now see what’s behind the mask! The official Incridea Mascot Comic is out now! 10 highly illustrated panels, 10s of painstaking hours, all to tell you the what, why & who for your Odyssey of a lifetime! meet RYOKO - The official mascot of Incridea 22, a traveler who travels beyond galaxies and time zones! So come along and join the adventure with RYOKO - this 12th, 13th, 14th & 15th of May. See you there. #incridea #odysseyofalifetime #comics 🚀🚀

Created by Nipun Sailan and the Illustration team, conceptualized by Wayne DSouza, Amogh Shet and Documentation team.
