## Overview

Incridea is NMAM Institute of Technology's national-level college fest. As
Design Committee Lead, I directed the brand identity and visual themes for the
event and owned the full attendee, sponsor, and visitor experience, with the
fest theme carried across digital, social media, and physical/print media.

I led this alongside freelance graphic design work and my Bachelor's degree in
Mechanical Engineering. The same systems-thinking habit that later carried
into UX and design systems work started here, in brand identity and event
experience design.

## Concept: Odyssey of a Lifetime

The 2022 theme, **Odyssey of a Lifetime**, ran on a portal motif: a recurring
image connecting the fest's past editions to the 2022 theme. Every touchpoint
that follows below carries that same portal image.

> **Figure:** `incridea-concept-portal.webp` — Odyssey of a Lifetime key
> visual: a portal motif connecting Incridea's past editions to the 2022 fest.
> *Caption: The portal key visual — the theme's central image, carried across
> every deliverable that follows.*

### RYOKO and the comic

The theme needed a face, so I designed **RYOKO**, an intergalactic traveler,
as the fest's mascot: a character who steps through the portal instead of
standing next to it. RYOKO's story was told
across a 10-panel comic strip, illustrated with the rest of the creative team,
that gave the abstract "odyssey" idea an actual character attendees could
follow.

## Bringing the theme to life

Odyssey of a Lifetime had to hold up across every format the fest actually
ran on: social feeds building awareness before the event, merch and crew
apparel worn during it, and certificates handed out after.

### Social & sponsor awareness

The pre-fest Instagram push centered on a before/after campus-transformation
carousel: the same location shown as an ordinary campus day, then as fest
day, built to make the scale of the transformation legible to sponsors
scrolling a feed.

### Merchandise

Participant merch was designed as everyday pieces someone would actually
reach for again after the fest, not costume-like fest-only apparel.

> **Figure:** `incridea-merch.webp` — Incridea 2022 participant merchandise
> carrying the Odyssey of a Lifetime theme.
> *Caption: Participant merchandise — designed for everyday wear beyond the
> fest.*

### Crew shirts

Crew apparel ran as its own line, separate from participant merch. Its job
was team identity for the volunteers and committee running the fest, not a
takeaway souvenir for attendees.

> **Figure:** `incridea-crew-shirts.webp` — Incridea 2022 crew and core team
> shirt design.
> *Caption: Crew shirts — team identity for the volunteers and committee
> running the fest.*

### Certificates

Participants, winners, volunteers, and committee members each got a
certificate designed for what they'd actually done, instead of a single
generic template stamped with a different name.

> **Figure:** `incridea-certificates.webp` — Incridea 2022 certificate
> designs for participants, winners, volunteers, and committee recognition.
> *Caption: Certificate set — participant, winner, volunteer, and
> committee/special recognition.*

## Outcome

The fest drove $35k in sponsorship, roughly 10x the historical average, with
the brand and creative direction credited as part of what made the pitch
land. A sponsor scrolling the campus-transformation carousel and a volunteer
picking up their crew shirt were looking at the same portal image, carried
from the key visual through to the certificates handed out at the end.
